# Section Playbook

Each CV section answers a different question. Match the verb palette and the thing you quantify to
the section's job. Frequencies below are from ~80,000 real bullets across ~2,000 IIM summers CVs.

---

## Header line (top of CV)

Format used by 95%+ of the corpus, pipe-separated:

```
Full Name | PGP <roll no.> | <Gender>, <Age>
```

Examples: `Aditya Vijay Jain | PGP 25011 | Male, 23` · `Kavya Akanksha Barwa | PGP 25135 | Female, 20`

---

## Section order

Freshers / campus hires lead with **Education** (84% of CVs start here). Candidates with strong work
experience may lead with **Work Experience**. Typical full order:

1. Education
2. Scholastic / Academic Achievements (& Certifications)
3. Work / Professional Experience
4. Internships
5. Projects / Research
6. Positions of Responsibility (PoR)
7. Extra-Curricular Activities

Section headers are written in CAPS. Common spellings seen: `EDUCATION` / `EDUCATIONAL BACKGROUND`,
`SCHOLASTIC ACHIEVEMENTS` / `ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENTS & CERTIFICATIONS`, `WORK EXPERIENCE` /
`PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE`, `POSITIONS OF RESPONSIBILITY`, `EXTRA-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES`.

---

## Side-labels (the left-margin tags)

Within a multi-bullet block, the corpus groups 1-4 bullets under a short **thematic label** set to the
left. Labels are 1-3 words. When drafting a full block, group bullets this way. Most common labels:
*Impact, Achievements, Leadership, Initiatives, Project, Strategy, Social Impact, Process, Analysis,
Recognition, Mentorship, Operations, Key Projects, Highlights, Role, Responsibilities.*

---

## EDUCATION

**Job:** establish pedigree and rank fast. This is a table, not bullets.

**Row format:** `Degree | Institution (Branch) | Score | Distinguishing highlight | Year`

```
B.Tech      IIT Bombay (Mechanical Major)     9.67/10     10/10 in 36/54 courses        2023
Class XII   Allen, Maharashtra State Board    90.15%      3rd in School                 2019
Class X     Delhi Public School, CBSE         96.4%       10/10 in all subjects         2017
```

**Highlight column** is where you quantify distinction: `Department Rank 1`, `Minor in CSE (9.0/10)`,
`≥90% in 5/6 subjects`, `Silver Medalist`, `Top 5% of batch`. Always include board (CBSE/ICSE/State)
and the score in the school's native scale (CGPA/10 or %).

---

## SCHOLASTIC / ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENTS

**Job:** prove selectivity and academic firepower. **Rarity is the metric.**

**Lead verbs:** Secured, Achieved, Awarded, Ranked, Scored, Received, Attained, Cleared, Qualified,
Won. (Top openers in corpus: Secured 971, Achieved 774, Awarded 579, Ranked 362, Scored 328.)

**Quantify with:** percentile (`99.28%ile`), All India Rank (`AIR 604`), out-of-pool size
(`out of 2.93L candidates`), selection ratio (`1/12`), exam name (CAT/XAT/JEE/GATE/GMAT/GRE/CFA).

**Real examples:**
- `Secured 99.97%ile in CAT (VARC 99.8, QA 99.92) - top 100 out of 2.93 lakh candidates`
- `KVPY SX: AIR 144 | JEE Advanced: AIR 604 | JEE Mains: AIR 758 out of 1.1+ million candidates`
- `Awarded IQAC scholarship: 1/12 in English Dept. for academic excellence & leadership`
- `Bronze medalist, International Olympiad of English (Int'l Rank 249)`

Common sub-labels: *Academic, Competitions, Competitive Exams, Olympiads, Scholarships, Certifications.*

---

## WORK / PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

**Job:** show business impact - money, time, efficiency, risk. This is the most scrutinised section.

**Block header:** `Company, Location    Role    (Mon'YY-Mon'YY)` with total tenure noted by the section title.

**Lead verbs:** Led, Reduced, Drove, Built, Delivered, Saved, Managed, Improved, Boosted, Automated,
Spearheaded, Enabled, Increased, Streamlined, Generated. (Impact + build verbs dominate here.)

**Quantify with:** currency (~1 in 5 bullets here carry Rs/$/EUR), % change, x-multiples, time saved,
scale of book/portfolio/users handled. State the *business* outcome, then the method.

**Real examples:**
- `Saved Rs2Cr/year, boosted plant production 2% through advanced data analysis of process recovery`
- `Reduced false alerts by 50% in transaction-monitoring rule with Financial Investigation Unit`
- `Built trading & pricing tool for EUR45bn+ book across swaps, swaptions, bonds & futures in 3 currencies`
- `Drove 18% YoY revenue increase (Rs5Cr) via insights from a 5-state, 8-client tracker study`

Common side-labels: *Impact, Client Impact, Process Optimisation, Recognition & Achievements,
Leadership, Market Research.*

---

## INTERNSHIPS

**Job:** show you delivered something real in 2-3 months, ideally with a PPO/PPI or LOR signal.

**Lead verbs:** Achieved, Developed, Conducted, Built, Designed, Analysed, Increased, Boosted, Reduced,
Engineered. (Corpus top: Achieved 311, Developed 210, Conducted 164, Reduced 157.)

**Quantify with:** % improvement, units processed, prototype performance, adoption. Note PPO/PPI/LOR
as a credibility line ("Received PPO based on internship performance").

**Real examples:**
- `Boosted in-store revenue 15% & footfall 10% in three weeks via localized marketing at a retail shop`
- `Engineered low-cost, no-capex translucent soap variant maintaining product standards (LUX, HUL)`
- `Increased sign-up rates 37% by revamping onboarding flow post user interviews with 50+ workers`
- `Reduced parameter count 96% with a 49k-param model achieving state-of-the-art score (0.981)`

---

## PROJECTS / RESEARCH

**Job:** show technical depth and a measurable result. Method + number.

**Lead verbs:** Built, Developed, Designed, Achieved, Analysed, Created, Evaluated, Implemented, Modelled.

**Quantify with:** accuracy/precision %, x-speedup, dataset size, error reduction. Name the technique or
tool (Python, Aspen, Heston model, FFT, SIEM) - specificity reads as competence here.

**Real examples:**
- `Achieved 98.6% model accuracy, reducing previous errors by 22% and improving prediction reliability`
- `Spearheaded barrier-option pricing for 30x speedup using Heston model & Fast Fourier Transform`
- `Built reusable tools enabling 200+ system studies faster; cut future setup time 60% across projects`
- `Achieved CAGR of 18.46% (2007-23) vs 9.52% SENSEX using higher-order-moment optimization in Python`

Research blocks often add a one-line credibility note: `Selected to present at <conference>` or
`Under guidance of Prof. <name>, <dept>`.

---

## POSITIONS OF RESPONSIBILITY (PoR)

**Job:** show leadership *scale* - how many people, how much money, how big an event. **Size is the metric.**

**Lead verbs:** Led, Managed, Organized, Spearheaded, Coordinated, Mentored, Elected, Raised, Secured,
Facilitated, Headed, Directed. (Corpus top: Led 1207, Managed 698, Organized 528, Spearheaded 491.)

**Quantify with:** team size led, budget/sponsorship raised (Rs), people/attendees impacted, events run,
selection ratio for the post (`1/23 selected out of 102`), % growth driven.

**Real examples:**
- `Led 80+ students as House Captain, securing top-3 rank in 5+ inter-house cultural & sports events`
- `Raised INR 70k sponsorship from 4+ partners, enabling a fully funded MUN without college funds`
- `Spearheaded a 15-member team & 2 committees, overseeing 100+ delegates at SPIT MUN`
- `1/23 selected from 102 applicants to Forum for Industry Interaction; 1/4 in Client Acquisition Cell`

Common side-labels: *Leadership, Initiatives, Mentorship, Operations, Social Impact, Recognition.*

---

## EXTRA-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES

**Job:** show distinction and breadth outside academics. **Rank and selectivity carry the line** -
this is the one section where many bullets need no method clause, just the result and its rarity.

**Lead verbs / openers:** Won, Secured, Ranked, Finalist, Winner, Volunteered, Represented, Mentored,
Captained, Bagged. (Corpus top: Winner 705, Secured 611, Volunteered 437, Won 318.)

**Quantify with:** position (`1st`, `Runner-Up`), selectivity (`1/12 selected`, `out of 90 teams`),
level (inter-school / national / state), participants reached.

**Real examples:**
- `Won WWF ECHO award (1/20 teams) at national summit; represented SRCC promoting sustainability`
- `Winners of Volleyball at Yalgaar, IIMA; 1 of 12 selected for the team`
- `Generated 115.7% equity & 98.5% options ROI by trading a self-managed portfolio`
- `Mentored 80+ third-year students in a 2-day AI & ML bootcamp`

Common side-labels by activity: *Sports, Quizzes, Case Comp, Debate, Social Service, Arts.*
