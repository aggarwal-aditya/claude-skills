# Verb Bank

Strong past-tense action verbs, grouped by what they do, with their frequency as a bullet's first
word across ~80,000 corpus bullets. Pick a verb that matches the section's job (see
section-playbook.md). Vary verbs within a block - never open two adjacent bullets with the same word.

## Status-noun openers (acceptable in Scholastic & Extra-Curricular only)
Some achievement lines open with a noun that *is* the achievement. This is idiomatic in the corpus
for awards/positions: `Winner` (377), `Member` (446), `Top` (362), `Finalist` (128), `National`,
`Captain`, `Recipient`. Use sparingly and only where the status itself is the headline.

---

## Banned / weak openers - rewrite these on sight
These signal a weak bullet. When you see them, find the real action verb underneath.

- "Responsible for ...", "Worked on ...", "Helped with / Helped to ...", "Involved in ...",
  "Tasked with ...", "Part of ...", "Assisted in ..." (Assisted is OK only for genuine support roles)
- Gerund openers: "Managing ...", "Leading ...", "Building ..." -> convert to past tense (Managed, Led, Built)
- First person: "I ...", "My ...", "We ..."
- Fluff adjectives/adverbs adding no information: "various", "successfully", "effectively",
  "different", "several" (replace with a number), "good", "many", "a lot of".

## Verb hygiene
- Past tense throughout (the work is done). Present tense only for an ongoing current role.
- One verb per bullet does the leading; don't stack "Led and managed and coordinated".
- Match verb strength to truth: "Supported" / "Contributed to" are honest for non-owning roles -
  don't upgrade to "Led" what you didn't lead.
